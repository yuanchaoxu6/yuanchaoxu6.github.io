I am an Assistant Professor of <font color="#0081ff">Department of Computer Science and Engineering</font> at <font color="#0081ff">University of California Santa Cruz</font>. Prior to that, I obtained my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from North Carolina State University (NCSU), advised by <font color="#0081ff">Prof. Xipeng Shen</font> and <font color="#0081ff">Prof. Yan Solihin</font>. Before that, I obtained my M.S. degree in Computer Science from Tsinghua University, advised by <font color="#0081ff">Prof. Wei Xue</font>, and my B.S. degree in Software Engineering from Jilin University. From the summer of 2021 to the end of 2022, I worked as a research intern at <font color="#0081ff">SystemResearch@Google</font>, where I was advised by <font color="#0081ff">Prof. David E. Culler</font>.
My research interests are in computer architecture, security, and ML systems and architecture.
I am always looking for self-motivated Ph.D. students interested in these areas. Feel free to contact me at yxu314@ucsc.edu if you are interested in working with me!
